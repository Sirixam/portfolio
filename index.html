I'm a game designer based in Santiago, with strong experience across combat, systems, and level design.

I've worked across PC and mobile titles, working hand-in-hand with engineers, artists and product managers to see my ideas go from concept to screen.

I combine creating clear, thorough documentation with a player-first design approach, actively listening to feedback, diagnosing core issues, and delivering focused solutions that solve problems without feature bloat.

During my free time I enjoy creating games while learning about different disciplines (game design, programming, 3D art & music composition). I also play widely across genres to stay inspired, though RPGs and Metroidvanias remain my favorites.

Currently open to freelance and full-time opportunities.

How I Work

01
Discover

I start with the player. What emotions should they feel? What fantasy does the game fulfill? Brainstorm, research, and player psychology set the foundation.

02
Design

Core loops, mechanics, and systems mapped before a single line of code. GDDs, flowcharts, and diagrams that keep the whole team aligned.

03
Iterate

Playtesting, metrics, and feedback loops drive refinement. I follow the data without losing sight of feel, because great games are both measured and felt.

04
Deliver

I build tools and automate repetitive work to cut down tedious overhead, so the team can spend more time on the game itself.
